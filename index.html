Local-first

Your work lives on your computer.

FlowJoe is a desktop app first. Your flows are saved on your own computer, you can keep any of them in a normal folder you choose, and the app keeps working with the internet switched off. We built it that way so your ideas never depend on our servers.

  • Stays on your drive

    Notes, pictures, media, documents and Joe conversations are saved on your computer. Your account never stores them.

  • Works offline

    Write, organise and open everything without a connection. Online features connect when you use them, apart from the automatic update checks listed below.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, no usage data, no crash reports. If usage stats ever arrive, they will be opt-in and off by default.

  • Yours to keep

    No account needed. Keep any flow in a folder you choose as plain flow.json and ordinary media files. Stopping a paid plan only pauses live sharing.

What does go online

  • Update checks to files.flowjoe.app every few hours, which see ordinary connection details such as your IP address
  • AI providers you choose, who receive the context you send to Joe
  • Live sharing, only while you host a session
  • Your account, when you sign in, send a report or vote on an idea
