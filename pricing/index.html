How sharing works

Your flow stays home. Guests visit.

  1. Start Pro

    FlowJoe sets up your personal sharing address. There is nothing to configure on your router.

  2. Invite people

    Send a link. Guests open it in their browser, with no account and no install.

  3. Host the session

    Your flow stays on your computer. Guests see it live only while you host, and you decide who can edit.

Questions

Before you choose

What happens to my work if I stop paying?

Nothing. Your flows, files and history stay on your computer and keep working. Only live sharing pauses.

Do my guests need to pay or sign up?

No. Guests join from a link in their browser. On Pro, up to three guests can edit at once, and anyone else can watch.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 7-day Pro trial needs no card. When it ends, your work stays on your computer and only live sharing pauses.

Is my project stored in the cloud?

No. Your flows and media stay on your computer. Your account holds sign-in, device, plan and sharing details, never your flows or media. When you share, guests receive what you share while the session is open. When you use online AI, the context you send goes to that provider.

Should I pick a Pass or Pro?

A Pass suits a single project review. If you share most months, Pro costs less over a year and keeps your address open.
