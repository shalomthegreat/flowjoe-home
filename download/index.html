Before you open it

First launch, one extra click.

Opening FlowJoe the first time

This alpha isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks once before opening it. Signed builds arrive before the public launch. Step by step: Install FlowJoe.

  1. MacOpen the .dmg and drag FlowJoe into Applications. Open it once and click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  2. WindowsIf you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. LinuxMake the AppImage runnable with chmod +x FlowJoe-*.AppImage, then open it.
